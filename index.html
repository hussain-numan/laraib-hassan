How I Work

A simple process. A serious build.

The same four steps, every project — from first conversation to production.

01

Understand

Before writing code, I understand the users, business goals, workflows, and technical requirements.

02

Design

I translate the product vision or Figma designs into a clear, responsive interface and reusable component system.

03

Build
