Common questions

A few things worth knowing before you reach out.

Do you work directly with clients?

Yes. Projects can be delivered directly to clients or through agency partnerships. Either way, communication remains direct and collaborative.

What kind of products do you build?

Web applications, SaaS platforms, mobile applications, booking systems, dashboards, admin panels, business software, and API-driven products.

How long does a project take?

Every project is different. Timeline depends on the scope, number of features, integrations, design readiness, and technical complexity. A realistic timeline can be discussed after understanding the requirements.

Do you provide design?

The primary focus is development and Figma-to-code implementation. If a project requires complete UI/UX design from scratch, a design partner can be brought into the process.

What happens after launch?

Ongoing support is available for maintenance, bug fixes, improvements, new features, integrations, and continued product development.

What industries have you worked in?

Creator technology, healthcare, HR technology, and EdTech/mentorship.
