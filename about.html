The stack keeps changing — AI integrations, Next.js, cloud DevOps — but the question I ask at the start of every project hasn't: build the thing that actually solves the problem, not the thing that looks impressive in a demo.

Good engineering starts with good questions.

Before deciding how something should be built, I want to understand:

  • Who will use it?
  • What problem are they trying to solve?
  • What currently makes that process difficult?
  • What absolutely needs to exist on day one?
  • What should be ready to scale later?

That mindset helps keep development focused on the product rather than the technology for its own sake.
